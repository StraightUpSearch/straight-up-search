A quick guide to the four "hats" in SEO and what each one means for the business paying for it.

| Hat | Approach | Speed | Risk |

|---|---|---|---|

| White | Inside Google's guidelines | Slow to start | Low, durable |

| Grey | On the line; some tactics Google dislikes | Faster | Medium |

| Black | Direct manipulation: link schemes, cloaking | Fastest spikes | High; penalties and crashes |

| Green | New and learning | Varies | Mostly from inexperience |

## The point

When you hire SEO you also hire a risk appetite. Ask which hat your provider wears before you ask what they promise.

## Our position

White hat, with a commercial focus. We match the approach to each business's goals and risk tolerance, and we aim for growth that survives the next algorithm update.
