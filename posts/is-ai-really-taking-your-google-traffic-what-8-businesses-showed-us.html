Is AI Really Taking Your Google Traffic? What 8 Businesses Showed Us

November 11, 2026
A short findings note on whether AI answers are taking Google clicks from ordinary businesses. ## Method 8 businesses we work with. The same 8 summer weeks in 2025 and 2026. Non brand clicks only, from Search Console. Question searches isolated with a regex on question words. ## Findings | Measure | Result | |---|---| | Question searches as a share of visible non brand clicks | Under 3% for 5 of 8; median 2% | | Question clicks year on year | Fell on 4, held or grew on 4 | | Question clicks worse than the rest of the site | 2 of 8 | | "best" and "vs" searches | Fell on 4 of the 5 with enough volume to measure | ## Reading it The searches AI answers best are a small part of these sites' Google traffic, and they mostly held up. The traffic that matters commercially comes from product and category searches, so that's where to look first when clicks fall. The "best" and "vs" drop is worth watching, but some samples are small. ## Limits Eight businesses over eight weeks. Search Console hides rare searches, which lean towards long questions, so the real question share is likely a little above 2%. Click data can't show people who got an AI answer and never searched. ## Regex ``` ^(what|how|why|can|do|does|is|are|should|when|which)\b ```
