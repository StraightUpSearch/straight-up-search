## Summary

"GEO" (generative engine optimisation) is being sold as a new discipline. In practice it is SEO applied to additional surfaces: LLM based answer engines.

## What changed in weighting

| Factor | Why it matters more now |

|---|---|

| Entities | LLMs reason in entities rather than strings |

| Citations on trusted surfaces | Reddit, YouTube and niche forums feed retrieval |

| Schema | The structured layer LLMs prefer |

| Internal linking | Still distributes authority to commercial pages |

## What didn't change

The work itself. Every item above has been part of competent SEO for years.

## Readiness check

- [ ] Organisation, Product, Article and FAQ schema in place

- [ ] Entities reinforced across the top 20 pages

- [ ] Presence on Reddit, niche communities and YouTube

- [ ] Internal linking depth on commercial pages

All four solid means the site is already optimised for AI answers. A separate GEO budget isn't needed.
