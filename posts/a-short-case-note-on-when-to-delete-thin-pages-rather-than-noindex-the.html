A short case note on when to delete thin pages rather than noindex them.

## Background

Our long standing advice was to noindex thin pages rather than delete them. A SaaS site with 1,800 noindexed location pages in its sitemap showed us where that advice breaks down.

## What we did

| Action | Pages |

|---|---|

| Noindexed location pages found | 1,800 |

| Deleted | 1,400 |

| Kept and rewritten (real local demand) | 400 |

## Results after three months

| Measure | Change |

|---|---|

| Page one keywords on rewritten pages | +41% |

| Crawl frequency on commercial pages | 3x |

| Total organic traffic | +22% |

## When noindex is still right

Pages that serve users but shouldn't rank: account pages, filter URLs, internal search results.

## When to delete

Pages that exist only because a project, often programmatic, was started and abandoned.

## Decision rule

Noindexed, with no realistic path back into the index within 90 days: deletion candidate.
