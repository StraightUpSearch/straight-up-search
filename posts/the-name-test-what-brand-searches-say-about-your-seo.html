The Name Test: What Brand Searches Say About Your SEO

November 8, 2026
Before we change anything on a new client's site, we check one number: what share of their Google clicks came from people who already knew the business name. This summer we pulled it for 8 of the businesses we work with. ## The numbers - **Median:** 8% of Google clicks came from brand searches. - **Top end:** three well known names in their markets sat above 40%. - **Bottom end:** four were under 6%. - **Trend:** brand clicks per day were down on all 8 against the same weeks last summer. So on a typical site, more than 90% of Google clicks came from people searching for a product, a problem or a question. ## What it means for reporting Brand clicks are driven mostly by reputation, advertising and word of mouth. Non brand clicks are what SEO is paid to grow. They move for different reasons, so they need reporting separately. When they're blended, a strong month for the brand can hide a weak month for the SEO, and the reverse. Every report we send splits the two for that reason. ## How we pull it In Search Console's Performance report, sites with enough volume now get a branded queries filter. It includes branded product searches, so it reads higher than a filter on the name alone. Where it isn't available, we add a Custom (regex) Query filter with the business name and every misspelling we can find. On one site this summer, the misspellings alone were worth over 250 clicks that would otherwise have been credited to SEO. Anonymised queries Google hides for privacy we count as non brand. They're almost never someone typing the business name. If your reports don't show this split, it's worth asking for.
