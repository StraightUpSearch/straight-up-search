A note on why sensible SEO recommendations stall, and a short checklist for getting them live.

## Why they stall

| Blocker | What it looks like |

|---|---|

| Taste | "I don't like that word in the title" |

| Ownership | Nobody knows who can approve it |

| Risk | Template changes feel dangerous |

| Priorities | Always pushed to next month |

## What gets them through

- Explain the problem before the fix.

- Agree the principle first, the exact wording second.

- Give two options rather than one.

- Propose a small, reversible test with a review date.

- Confirm who approves what, in writing.

- Track recommendations to live, not just to approved.

Most blocked work isn't wrong. It's stuck. Treat getting it live as part of the job.
